Remote Job Radar

A personal job-search assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

What it does

Remote Job Radar collects remote job postings from public job feeds, filters them for a single candidate's skills and location, and prepares application material. It also keeps that one user informed about the application process by reading their own Gmail inbox and by adding interview appointments to their own Google Calendar.

Who it is for

It is a private, single-user tool. There is no sign-up, no accounts, and no other users. It is not distributed as a product and it can only be run by the person who operates it.

How Google data is used

  • Gmail (read-only): to detect replies, rejections and interview invitations for applications the owner already submitted.
  • Google Calendar: to create calendar events for interviews the owner has been invited to.
  • Nothing is sent to the owner's contacts, no email is sent or deleted, and no calendar data is shared.

Where data lives

All data stays on the owner's computer in a local database file. Messages are processed by a language model running locally on the same machine. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server, and no data is sold or used for advertising.
